What is the difference between the two cleaners?

The 5-IN-1 uses a citric-acid-based formula for many common kitchen and bathroom surfaces. Stone Pro Plus is marketed for natural stone and more delicate surfaces. Always check each product's directions and surface cautions before use.

Do I use the concentrate straight from the bottle?

No. These are concentrated products. Dilute with water using the instructions on the specific product label or product page.

Can I use the 5-IN-1 on natural stone or polished wood?

The product page says not to use the 5-IN-1 on unsealed natural stone, polished wood, some painted surfaces or delicate surfaces. For natural stone, see Stone Pro Plus and test a small area first.

What if my order arrives damaged?

The store FAQ asks customers to contact Cassell Cleaners promptly about damaged orders. For help with an order, email cassellcleaners@gmail.com.
